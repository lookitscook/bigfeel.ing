Big Feeling is emotion electric.

Use quantified sentiment to direct
the feel of your digital experiences.

Film captures a moment. Binary bits capture knowledge. Big Feeling captures emotion. Your digital experiences are not their codecs, resolutions, and hex colors. Your brand, your enterprise, your people are more than typefaces and click-throughs. They are feelings.

Manage assets and define standards based directly on those feelings. Ingest insights and normalize emotions generated by the experiences you manage, providing natural access to people, machine agents, and friendly external services. Quantitatively test to reach qualitative sentiment goals.

For people-facing digital experiences, Big Feeling provides the JPEG, MP4, and DMX you need (or AVIF, HAPQ, NDI if you prefer). Online or completely cut off from society in the woods or at a tradeshow, our caching and playback applications enable touchpoints as durable as the physical world they're used in.

Big Feeling also speaks EmotionML, A-SMIL, MediaRSS, MCP, and vanilla RESTful JSON to play nicely with your other friends.
